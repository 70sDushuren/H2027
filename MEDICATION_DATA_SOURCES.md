# Medication reference data policy

The in-app catalog is a data-entry aid, not a prescribing system. A catalog item may prefill a generic name and marketed strength, but the user must confirm every value against a prescription label or instructions from a clinician or pharmacist.

## Regional sources

- Mainland China: National Health Commission chronic-disease and primary hypertension standards.
- Hong Kong: Hospital Authority Drug Formulary and Schedule 2 specified-drug list.
- United States: National Library of Medicine DailyMed structured prescribing labels.
- Taiwan: Taiwan Food and Drug Administration electronic package-insert platform and medicine-use guidance.
- Singapore: Ministry of Health clinical guidelines and chronic-disease programme guidance.

## Inclusion rules

1. Use generic concepts rather than recommending brands.
2. Store regions, common marketed strengths, formulation notes, source URL, and review date.
3. Treat label dosing text as an example for transcription and confirmation, never as an automatic default prescription.
4. Do not infer dose from diagnosis, age, laboratory values, or another user's history.
5. Keep custom user entries separate from curated reference entries.
6. Re-review entries when official labels or formularies change.

The initial seed focuses on hypertension, type 2 diabetes, dyslipidemia, hypothyroidism, and gout. Additional conditions and medicines require the same source review before release.
